Privacy Policy

Atech Pool Service

This Privacy Policy explains how Atech Pool Service collects, uses and protects the information you provide when you contact us or request pool cleaning, repair, maintenance or remodeling services.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, which typically includes:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The service you are interested in (pool cleaning, pool repair or remodeling).
  • Any details about your pool or project that you include in your message.
  • Your messaging consent preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, schedule and carry out service visits, and keep you informed about work on your pool.

Text and Email Messages

If you check the transactional messages agreement, we may send you appointment reminders, order confirmations and account notifications. If you check the marketing agreement, we may also send special offers, discounts and service updates. Message frequency may vary and message & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent to marketing messages is optional and is never required to receive service.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with team members and service providers who need it to deliver the service you requested, or where we are required to do so by law.

Data Retention and Security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations, and we take reasonable measures to protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop sending you messages, by calling us at (469) 945-6922.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one that applies.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Call Atech Pool Service at (469) 945-6922, Monday to Friday, 09:00 AM – 05:00 PM.
